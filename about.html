About Our Club

AITU Football Club was created to give students a place to enjoy football, improve their skills, and stay active in a healthy campus environment.

Our players learn how to work as a team, stay focused on goals, and support each other on and off the field.

Our Mission

We aim to create a friendly and professional football experience for all students at Astana IT University. The club encourages discipline, communication, and a strong spirit of teamwork.

What We Value

  • Confidence through regular practice
  • Fair play in every training session
  • Leadership from every player
  • Unity and respect inside the club
