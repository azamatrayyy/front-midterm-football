About Our Club

AITU Football Club was created to give students a place to enjoy football, improve their skills, and stay active in a healthy campus environment.

Our players learn how to work as a team, stay focused on goals, and support each other on and off the field.

Our Mission

We aim to create a friendly and professional football experience for all students at Astana IT University. The club encourages discipline, communication, and a strong spirit of teamwork.

What We Value

  • Confidence through regular practice
  • Fair play in every training session
  • Leadership from every player
  • Unity and respect inside the club

Frequently Asked Questions

The club welcomes AITU students who enjoy football and want to improve their skills.

Our sample training schedule is Tuesday and Thursday, from 6:30 PM to 8:00 PM. Confirm the actual session with the team.

Players of different levels can take part in training. Tryouts help the team find suitable roles for each player.

Bring suitable football shoes, sports clothes, shin guards and a water bottle.

Visit the Matches page. Use the search field and category buttons to find friendly, league or cup matches.

The Home page includes Explore Football Clubs. Select Arsenal, Liverpool or Manchester United and load information about the club from TheSportsDB.
